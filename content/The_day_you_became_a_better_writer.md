本文主要翻译 ： 《The day you became a better writer》 -- Scott Adams

> _I went from being a bad writer to a good writer after taking a one-day course in “business writing.” I couldn’t believe how simple it was. I’ll tell you the main tricks here so you don’t have to waste a day in class._

在经历一天的"商业写作"课程后， 我从一个写作差生 转变为了一名写作好手。它简单得令我不敢置信。 我将告诉你主要得技巧，避免你在课程上浪费一天时间。

>_Business writing is about clarity and persuasion. The main technique is keeping things simple. Simple writing is persuasive. A good argument in five sentences will sway more people than a brilliant argument in a hundred sentences. Don’t fight it._

商务写作的关键在于**清晰和说服力**。主要技巧在于化繁为简。**简洁**的文字更具说服力。五句话的精辟论证比一百句话的华丽辞藻更能打动人心。不要抗拒这一点。

>_Simple means getting rid of extra words. Don’t write, “He was very happy” when you can write “He was happy.” You think the word “very” adds something. It doesn’t. Prune your sentences._

**简洁意味着去掉多余的词语。** 与其写“他非常高兴”，不如直接写“他很高兴”。你可能觉得“非常”这个词能起到画龙点睛的作用，但其实不然。精简你的句子吧。


>_Humor writing is a lot like business writing. It needs to be simple. The main difference is in the choice of words. For humor, don’t say “drink” when you can say “swill.”_

幽默写作和商务写作有很多相似之处，都需要简洁明了。主要区别在于用词。比如，写幽默文章时，能说“swill”（一饮而尽）就不要说“drink”（喝）。

> _Your first sentence needs to grab the reader. Go back and read my first sentence to this post. I rewrote it a dozen times. It makes you curious. That’s the key._

**你的第一句话必须抓住读者的眼球。** 回去看看我这篇文章的第一句话。我改了十几次才写出来。它能激起读者的好奇心。这才是关键。

>_Write short sentences. Avoid putting multiple thoughts in one sentence. Readers aren’t as smart as you’d think._

**尽量写短句。** 避免在一句话里塞入多个想法。读者的智商可能不如你想象的那么高。

> _Learn how brains organize ideas. Readers comprehend “the boy hit the ball” quicker than “the ball was hit by the boy.” Both sentences mean the same, but it’s easier to imagine the object (the boy) before the action (the hitting). All brains work that way. (Notice I didn’t say, “That is the way all brains work”?)_

**了解大脑如何组织信息。** 读者理解“男孩击球”比理解“球被男孩击中”更快。两句话意思相同，但先想象物体（男孩）再想象动作（击球）更容易。所有人的大脑都是这样运作的。（请注意，我并没有说“所有人的大脑都是这样运作的”）。

> _That’s it. You just learned 80% of the rules of good writing. You’re welcome._

好了，你已经掌握了优秀写作规则的80%。不用谢。
